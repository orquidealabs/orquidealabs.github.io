Delete your account

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In the app

Open Ungoon, go to Profile, tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email orquidealabs@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Ungoon account" and your username. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, email, username, streaks, slip log, check-ins and XP. This is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep nothing after deletion, except where the law requires it.
