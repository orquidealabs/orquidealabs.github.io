Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what Ungoon collects, why, and the choices you have. Ungoon has no ads, no analytics and no tracking of your activity in other apps.

What we collect

  • Email address and password. Used to create your account and log you in. Your email is never shown to other people. Your password is stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • Username. A name you choose. It is the only thing other people can see, and only if private mode is off. Do not use your real name.
  • Habit data. When your streak started and ended, slips you log and the optional context you pick (stress, boredom, night, tiredness), and your daily check-ins, panic-screen use and XP.
  • Settings. Whether private mode is on, and when you last changed your username.

Why

To run the app: show your streak and history, give you XP and levels, and build the public ranking. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

What other people can see

On the public ranking: your username and XP. With private mode on, you do not appear. Your streaks, slips and context are never shown to others.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our hosting and database provider. It is sent over encrypted connections. It is shared with no other third party, except where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile, Delete account). That permanently removes your account, streaks, log and XP. If you cannot open the app, see how to request deletion.

Age

Ungoon is not for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above, and for important changes we tell you in the app.
